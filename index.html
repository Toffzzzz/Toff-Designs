Bluebird Wellness

A doctor-led IV clinic in west London, in clinic or at your door.

What we built

  • A scroll story where one ink line draws each treatment
  • Sixteen treatment pages, each with prices and ingredients
  • An ingredients glossary and six plain-English research guides
  • A booking preview and a guided chat that asks the right questions
  • Privacy, cookie, complaints and accessibility pages

Everest Climate Solutions

Air conditioning, designed and fitted across London.

What we built

  • A product-launch style home page in blue and soft white
  • An air-conditioning unit that opens as the room cools from 32° to 26°

  • Clear services, quotes and contact on every screen
