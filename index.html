Your website can answer your customers for you

It can also do things for them: take an order, find a free time, book a visit.

Other AI we build into websites

  • Instant quotes from a few simple questions
  • Search that understands whole questions
  • Menu, product and treatment finders that ask what you’re after
  • Enquiries summarised and sent to the right person
  • Answers in your customers’ own languages
  • Draft replies to reviews and emails, for you to check and send

Every assistant says it’s an AI, has firm limits on what it will talk about, and can hand over to a real person.

To build yours, we need what you’d tell a new member of staff: your menu or price list, opening hours, policies and the questions customers ask most. When something changes, tell us and we update it.
