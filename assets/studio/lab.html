Lettering made of gold specks

    
A few thousand points, each pulled towards its place in a shape and pushed away by your pointer. The physics runs on the processor; the drawing runs on the graphics chip.

Your logo, drawn in one continuous line

      
Drop in your logo or a photo of your place. Your browser traces it as a single gold line and draws it, start to finish, without lifting the pen: logos as clean outlines, photos as shading. Nothing leaves your device.

A letter cast in liquid gold

      
There is no 3D model here: the T is described as a shape in maths and drawn pixel by pixel by the graphics chip. It ripples slowly and turns to face your pointer.
