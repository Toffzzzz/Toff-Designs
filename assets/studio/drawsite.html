Sketch your homepage and see how it could look

Drag blocks onto the paper (a menu bar, headings and text you can type into, buttons, pictures) and sketch with the pen if you like. When you’re happy, press “I’m done” and it’s turned into a homepage you can scroll through, in the style you picked above. It’s a preview of the layout; we build the real thing.
