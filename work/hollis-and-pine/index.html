      Delivery and returns

      How much is delivery?

      £4.95 anywhere in the UK, and free on orders over £120. Orders go out within two working days, wrapped in paper and card, never plastic.

      Can I collect from the shop?

      Yes, for free. Choose “Collect from the shop” at checkout and we’ll put your order aside for a week.

      Can I return something?

      Send it back within 30 days, unworn or unused, and we’ll refund you in full. Bring it to the shop, or post it back at your own cost.

      Why does my vase look a little different?

      Each piece is thrown and glazed by hand, so sizes, glaze lines and speckles vary a little from the pictures. We think that’s the point.
