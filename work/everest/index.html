What Everest needed

A home page that shows what air conditioning does: a warm room turning comfortable.

Everest designs and fits air conditioning across London. Its website had to make that feeling visible on the home page, then keep the practical things close: what Everest offers, how to get a quote and how to get in touch, on every screen.

It started as a sketch

Every site we make starts on paper. At the centre of this one is the unit on the wall, with the temperature going from 32° to 26°.

The notes in its margins

  • “Clean, simple header”
  • “Maybe sticky?”
  • “Primary CTA”
  • “Show comfort + wellbeing”
  • “Key services”
  • “Icons? Keep simple”

The finished home page plays that moment as you scroll: the unit opens and the room cools, one degree at a time.

What we built

  • A product-launch style home page in blue and soft white
  • An air-conditioning unit that opens as the room cools from 32 °C to 26 °CThe flap opens as the number counts down in whole degrees, and the number turns from warm to cool.
  • A hand-drawn wind line that sweeps across the page
  • Clear services, quotes and contact on every screen
