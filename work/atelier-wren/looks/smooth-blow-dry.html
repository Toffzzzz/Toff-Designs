The smooth blow-dry

Blow-dry, £35, 45 minutes. Often by Sam.

Washed, then dried smooth with a large round brush, the ends turned under.

The finish

Polished but still soft to touch, with no stiffness from product.

Keeping it

It lasts two or three days. Sleep on a silk pillowcase, and a dry shampoo at the roots on the second morning.
