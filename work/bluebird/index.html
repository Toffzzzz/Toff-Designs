What the clinic needed

Sixteen treatments to explain, each with its price and what goes into it.

Bluebird treats people at its clinic in west London or at their door, and every treatment is led by its doctors. The website had to set out each treatment with its price and ingredients, show the research behind them in plain English, and make it easy to start a booking or ask a question first.

It also needed privacy, cookie, complaints and accessibility pages. And it had to feel calm.

It started as a sketch

Every site we make starts on paper. Here the idea is already on the page: one line running from an IV bag, through a face, down to a coconut and a bird.

The notes in its margins

  • “Simpler logo?”
  • “Hero section: calm, clean, open”
  • “Science backed?”
  • “Maybe photo instead of illustration?”
  • “Ingredients? Sustainability? Icons?”

The finished site kept the line, drawn in navy, and kept the illustrations. Two of the notes ask about the science and the ingredients; the site has an ingredients glossary and six plain-English research guides.

What we built

  • A scroll story where one ink line draws each treatmentA single navy line runs down the home page and draws an iron drip, a face, a coconut and a weightlifter as you scroll.
  • Sixteen treatment pages, each with prices and ingredientsThe home page ends with all of them on one menu.
  • An ingredients glossary and six plain-English research guides
  • A booking preview and a guided chat that asks the right questions
  • Privacy, cookie, complaints and accessibility pages
