What happens on the day

The same way every time, so you know where you stand.

  1. Before we set off

    You get a text when we’re on the way, with the name of whoever’s coming and a rough arrival time inside your window.

  2. When we arrive

    Dust sheets down and shoe covers on. We look first, then tell you what we’ve found in plain words.

  3. Before any work starts

    You hear the price for the job, from the list where there is one. Nothing starts until you’ve said yes.

  4. Before we leave

    We test everything we’ve touched, tidy up and show you what we did, so you know what to look out for.

Questions and answers

Which areas do you cover?

West London, from Paddington out to Ealing: the postcode districts W2 to W14. If you’re not sure, check your postcode on the map.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. On Sundays we do emergency call-outs only.

How much is a boiler service?

£95. It’s a yearly service, with a full safety check.

What does an emergency call-out cost?

£120, then £60 an hour. Ring 020 7946 0742 any time for leaks, no heating or no hot water.

Do you charge to come and quote?

No. A quote visit is free. Anything bigger than the jobs on our price list, like a new bathroom or a new boiler, gets a free quote visit.

I can smell gas. What should I do?

Call the gas emergency line on 0800 111 999 first. Open doors and windows, turn the gas off at the meter if you can, don’t use switches or flames, and wait outside. Then ring us.

Is the instant price what I’ll pay?

It’s a guide worked out from our price list. Fixed prices, like the boiler service, stay fixed. For everything else we agree the price with you on the day, before any work starts.
