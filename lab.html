The Lab

Experiments in what a web browser can draw by itself. They aren’t on client sites unless you ask for one, but any of them could be.

  • Sketch to site

    Drop in a screenshot of any website, watch it turn into a sketch, then paint it back. Good for: showing a redesign

  • Your initials in liquid gold

    Type up to three letters and they’re cast in molten gold that turns to face you. Good for: jewellers and luxury brands

  • A million specks

    Gold specks gather into the name you type, and your pointer or finger pushes them about. Good for: launches and events

  • Shape a vase

    Draw half of a vase’s outline and it turns into a vase you can glaze and photograph. Good for: makers and shops

  • Ink that moves

    Stir gold and cream ink through midnight water, and the pen writes your name into it. Good for: artisans and studios

  • Letters with weight

    Type a word and its letters drop, stack and can be thrown. Good for: playful brands

  • Your logo in one line

    Drop in a logo and watch it drawn as one continuous line, without the pen lifting. Good for: brand moments

  • The making of this site

    Scrub through the rounds this site was built in, from the first version to this one. Good for: showing a client how a site grows
